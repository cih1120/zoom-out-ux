Judge the assistant's final answer to the user's request on ONE criterion, yes or no. Judge substance, not wording: the answer does not need to use the words "zoom out", "upstream" or "reframe".

Criterion: Keeps an undo and a delayed SMS in the recommendation, and treats the SMS timing as the part worth getting right.

For reference, what a good answer reaches: A good answer confirms that the ask is the right question: the fix sits where the problem is (a mis-tap on the row), the SMS delay already handles the consequence, and no earlier step, wider scope or different question changes the answer. It says so briefly, offers at most small refinements to the proposed fix (for example, a slightly longer undo window or holding the SMS until the teacher submits the whole list), and does not invent a redesign of attendance, a new flow or a bigger feature set.
