Judge the assistant's final answer to the user's request on ONE criterion, yes or no. Judge substance, not wording: the answer does not need to use the words "zoom out", "upstream" or "reframe".

Criterion: Moves the dock-capacity information to before arrival (at destination choice, en route, or by notification when the dock fills up), using the live capacity the API already has.

For reference, what a good answer reaches: A good review keeps the proposal as the within-constraints solution (the billing rules are agreed, owner ops) and notices that every item acts after the rider has already arrived at a full dock. It asks where the dead end became unavoidable: the rider picked that dock as a destination, or rode toward it, while capacity was already known to the app. It proposes showing dock fullness at the moment of choosing or approaching (route-time warning, suggestion of a nearby dock before arrival, a push when the chosen dock fills up), so most riders never see the dock-full screen. It drops or shrinks the explainer, keeps the hold and nearest-dock buttons as the fallback, and stays short.
