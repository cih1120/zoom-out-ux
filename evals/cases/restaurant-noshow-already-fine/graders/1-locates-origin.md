Judge the assistant's final answer to the user's request on ONE criterion, yes or no. Judge substance, not wording: the answer does not need to use the words "zoom out", "upstream" or "reframe".

Criterion: States, in substance, that the proposed fix sits where the problem occurs and that no redesign is needed; it may still refine the fix.

For reference, what a good answer reaches: A good answer confirms that the ask is the right question: the fix sits where the problem is (a mis-tap on the row), the held fee already handles the consequence, and no earlier step, wider scope or different question changes the answer. It says so briefly, offers at most small refinements to the proposed fix (for example, a longer undo window, or holding the fee until the end of service or until the host reviews the night's no-shows), and does not invent a redesign of the host stand, a new flow or a bigger feature set.
