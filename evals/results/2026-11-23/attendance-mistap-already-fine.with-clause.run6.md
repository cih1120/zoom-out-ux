Zoomed out: nothing; the ask is the right question. Your changes all sit on one error, the mis-tap, at the step where it happens, so they count as one fix.

Recommendation: ship it, with three changes.

- **Hold the SMS until the teacher submits the roll, or for a few minutes, instead of 5 seconds.** A hurried teacher on a loud bus may not see the toast. With a 5-second window, the mis-tap they don't notice still sends the SMS. If you keep the toast, make it per row, because toasts will stack as they mark students quickly. Showing "Absent" on the row, with a pending-SMS indicator, also lets them spot and fix the mistake later.
- **Make the gap more than 16 px, or separate Late and Absent by more than spacing.** Put Late in the middle and Absent at the far end, or make the targets bigger. Most mis-taps come from target size and adjacency, not gap alone. Aim for at least 44 px targets.
- **Test the outlined Absent style on a real phone, outdoors if you can.** An outlined button can read as "disabled" or "secondary", which could make teachers miss it when they do mean Absent.
